# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Hadi's LocaAnimo to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its community
- Owning mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing generated content that targets or demeans real people
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@abdulhadiadnan1080](https://github.com/abdulhadiadnan1080). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
